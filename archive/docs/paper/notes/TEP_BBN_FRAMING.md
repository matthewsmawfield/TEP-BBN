# TEP-BBN Strategic Framing

## 1. Relationship to the Published Cosmology Sequence (C0/HC/TH)

The existing cosmology sequence (C0, HC, and TH) establishes the **conservative temporal-conformal correspondence branch**: 
- **C0** demonstrates that observed redshift and distance can be reconstructed as conformal proper-time transport without requiring primitive spatial expansion.
- **HC** shows that the CMB acoustic scales, thermal scaling ($T \propto 1/A$), and recombination physics are preserved, meaning early-universe matter still samples a hotter effective background.
- **TH** explicitly removes the Big Bang singularity, replacing it with a temporal horizon where the observational clock-rate field vanishes, while showing that at BBN temperatures the dynamical response is fully screened.

Crucially, in this screened correspondence branch, **the standard BBN thermal history and predicted light-element abundances are preserved.**

## 2. The Specific Role of TEP-BBN

TEP-BBN does **not** contradict the correspondence branch established by C0/HC/TH. Instead, it tests whether the observational deuterium pillar remains invariant once the absorbing system itself is allowed to possess absorber-scale proper-time shear.

The remaining abundance pillar is not the nuclear existence of deuterium, but the inference that high-redshift D I absorption provides an invariant primordial baryometer. TEP-BBN asks:

> **Are the high-redshift absorption systems used to infer primordial D/H actually invariant under absorber-scale temporal shear?**

In the correspondence branch, standard D/H is preserved. In the **non-hot-origin branch** tested here, the high-redshift D/H inference may fail because apparent D I absorption can be reinterpreted as ordinary H I structure transported through a nontrivial temporal clock map. 

Therefore, TEP-BBN functions as the **deuterium invariance test of the FLRW/BBN interpretation**. It does not declare an immediate deuterium collapse, but opens the door to the Hoyle-compatible branch by challenging the invariance of the observational inference.

## 3. Verdict on NOEMA and the "Hot Origin"

NOEMA high-redshift CMB-temperature measurements prove that high-redshift matter samples a hotter effective microwave background. However, they do **not** uniquely prove that space primitively expanded from a physical hot singularity. HC and TH accept the temperature-redshift law while denying that it uniquely selects a physical Big Bang. TEP-BBN attacks the single remaining observational pillar—primordial deuterium—that protects the standard "hot origin" interpretation.
