Automated public archive discovery has been attempted using both alias-based and coordinate-based searches. The coordinate sweep resolved several missing targets through SIMBAD/NED and queried public ESO/KOA-style archive endpoints using 10-arcsec positional matching and Lyα wavelength-window validation. No additional public high-resolution UVES/HIRES spectra satisfying the required [-300,+100] km/s Lyα window were recovered. The remaining systems are therefore classified as requiring manual KOA/HIRES provisioning or explicit coordinate curation before scientific classification.
