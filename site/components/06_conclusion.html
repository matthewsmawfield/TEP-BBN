6. Conclusion

Across three benchmark D/H absorption systems, unrestricted H I is significantly preferred over isotope-tied D I within the tested Voigt architecture, including under adversarial parent reassignment and the primary multiplicity correction. Q1009+2956 and PKS\,1937$-$101 also show significant departures from exact isotope locking in the calibrated Gate-8 analysis, while J1332$+$0052 is less discriminating. The result therefore demonstrates that the deuterium interpretation is not spectroscopically unique within the tested model class; the isotope-tied model is strongly disfavoured relative to unrestricted H within the tested architecture.

The fixed $-81.6\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$ clustering is not an independent discriminator in a sample selected through the deuterium search window. The corresponding falsifiable population test is an excess of suitable displaced H I components outside that window. The archival pilot establishes the practical selection limits of this test and identifies one candidate feature in Q1009+2956, but a substantially larger usable sample is required.

TEP provides a candidate physical interpretation of this displaced-H phenomenology. The conformal absorber field gives the blueward sign but only a $0.005$--$0.01\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$ shift. A temporal-disformal channel can reach the observed scale in the explored parametrisation. The full covariant evaluation (Gate 10) shows that the earlier closure obstruction is a property of the imported $\Lambda$CDM drift rate rather than of the disformal channel: joint sign-and-closure compatibility requires a descent-type ambient drift ($d\ln H_T^2/d\ln(1+z) < 0$), which the unsourced free-roll on the reconstructed corpus potential supplies, and under that rate a descent-tracking coupling $B(\varphi) = B_0\,\varphi^4/(\varphi_c^4+\varphi^4)\,e^{-\varphi}$ passes Cassini and GW170817, with inverse roots agreeing to a factor 1.0001. Forward evaluation at that common $B_0$ does not reproduce the three amplitudes, and ambient-lapse self-consistency excludes the branch outright: the flat-$u$ realisation holds the ambient sector at near-criticality, where the matter-metric redshift map would require a sign-violating negative ambient field. The unscreened temporal-disformal channel is accordingly bounded sub-critical at these epochs. Independently, the baryonic-cycling calculation shows that a helium-4 equilibrium near the observed abundance is possible under the adopted VMO-dominated, metal-sequestering branch. The spectroscopic, population and light-element predictions therefore provide separate empirical routes for testing the proposed non-hot-BBN interpretation. The scope of the deuterium argument is bounded accordingly: the analysis establishes that the benchmark absorber identifications are non-unique, not that a deuterium production channel exists. In an eternal recycling cosmology the distinction is binding — astration is a pure deuterium sink, so the standing Galactic interstellar value ${\rm D/H} \approx 1.5$--$2\times10^{-5}$, measured independently of the quasar-absorber architecture criticised here, is a production requirement rather than a relic; galactic-cosmic-ray spallation, pre-main-sequence envelope recycling, and pristine inflow each fail to supply it. The extended ledger (Step 15, results/gate15\_deuterium\_extended\_channels.json) quantifies three channels that operate before or beside temporal-well formation — VMO partial-burning/freezeout ejecta, the collapse neutrino burst irradiating the envelope through near-null neutrinos on the matter metric, and protected-reservoir accumulation on bounded-exposure worldlines — of which the first and third can close the required net yield within documented ranges — at a transit fraction $f_{\rm ej} = 0.5$ the ejecta must reach $X_D \ge 7.5\times10^{-5}$ if the feed carries ambient ISM deuterium — reducing the open item to a VMO ejecta-enrichment calculation and a reservoir mixing rate rather than a missing mechanism. Independently, the opacity three-point requirement is shown to admit an explicit species-resolved profile (Step 14, results/step\_14\_opacity\_three\_point.json): the wing datum fixes $n_e \approx 0.51\,n_{b,0}$, the intervening trough is derived to be neutral ($\bar n_e < 3.7\times10^{-4}\,n_{b,0}$), and the ionization front at the Gate-11B thermalization depth places the $\tau = 1$ surface at $z \approx 1080$. The boundary's self-consistency items are then closed at toy-model level (Step 18, results/step\_18\_boundary\_selfconsistency.json): the $\sim 4\times10^{10}$ wing-to-trough ionization-rate contrast is supplied by self-shielding over $\sim 24$ LyC e-folds; the front gas temperature is derived ($T_{\rm gas} = T_{\rm bath} = 2946$\,K via Compton equilibrium on a $\sim$yr coupling time), making the Saha ionization a consequence of the Gate-11B bath rather than an assumption; the visibility tail is shown to be a closure artifact removed when the edge is absorptive; and the deuterium channels' residual budgets are bounded (transit neutrino irradiation under a stated homologous-collapse model, reservoir longevity reduced to a $\sim 10^{3}$--$10^{4}$\,Gyr sequestration requirement). The remaining open item in the opacity sector is the perturbation mode spectrum on the boundary — the eternal-native Boltzmann treatment — together with the absorber-statistics inversion on a full LLS/Lyman-forest catalogue, which is the identified pipeline extension. The eternal-cosmology reading also inherits a caveat registered in the corpus's homogeneous analysis: the closed static background is not a dynamical attractor — its linearization exhibits an Eddington-type secular mode with e-folding $\sim a/c$ — and control of the analogous mode in the inhomogeneous realization remains part of the open construction (Paper 0, §8); the asymptotic-equilibrium framework evaluated here is accordingly a statement about candidate chemical attractors on a background whose global stability is asserted rather than yet demonstrated.
